# Creating Your Account

## Overview

New users can create an Alonix account by signing up with email or by accepting an invitation to join a workspace. This guide walks through self-service signup and email verification.

## Purpose

Get your account active so you can log in, join workspaces, and start working with documents.

## Prerequisites

- A valid work or personal email address (per your organization's policy)
- Access to that email inbox for verification
- Supported browser — see [System Requirements](/docs/getting-started/system-requirements)

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. Open the signup page

1. Go to your organization's Alonix URL (e.g., `https://app.alonix.example`).
2. Click **Sign up** or **Create account** on the login screen.

[Screenshot – Login page with Sign up link highlighted]

### 2. Enter your details

Fill in the registration form:

1. **Full name** — displayed in activity logs and user lists.
2. **Email address** — used for login and notifications.
3. **Password** — follow the strength requirements shown on screen.

Click **Create account** or **Sign up**.

### 3. Verify your email

1. Check your inbox for a message from Alonix.
2. Click the **Verify email** link in the message.
3. You will be redirected to Alonix with your email confirmed.

:::tip No email within 5 minutes?
Check spam/junk folders and confirm you entered the correct address.
:::

### 4. Complete your profile (optional)

After verification, you may be prompted to:

- Set a profile photo
- Confirm time zone or display preferences
- Accept terms of service (SaaS)

See [Profile Settings](/docs/user-guide/profile-settings) for ongoing profile management.

### 5. Join or create a workspace

Depending on your deployment:

- **Invited users** — you land directly in the workspace you were invited to.
- **Self-signup** — you may need a Company Admin to assign you to a Group, or you may create your first workspace if permitted.

Follow [Your First Workspace](/docs/getting-started/your-first-workspace) next.

## Expected Results

- Your account exists and email is verified.
- You can log in with your email and password.
- You appear in your organization's user list (visible to admins).
- You have access to at least one workspace, or know whom to contact for access.

## Tips

- Use a work email if your company standardizes on domain-based access.
- Save your Alonix URL as a bookmark after first login.
- If you were invited, use the **same email** the invitation was sent to.

## Best Practices

- Use a unique, strong password or your company's approved password manager.
- Complete email verification promptly—some features stay locked until verified.
- Do not share accounts; each person should have their own login for audit trails.

## Common Mistakes

- Signing up with a personal email when your org requires `@company.com`.
- Clicking an expired verification link—request a new verification email from the login page.
- Creating a duplicate account after receiving an invite—use **Accept invitation** instead.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| "Email already registered" | Use [Logging In](/docs/getting-started/logging-in) or [Forgot password](#forgot-password). |
| Verification link expired | Request a new verification email from the signup/login screen. |
| No workspace after signup | Contact your Company Admin or the person who invited you. |
| Signup disabled | Your org may be invite-only—ask an admin for an invitation. |

### Forgot password

If you already have an account but forgot your password, use **Forgot password** on the login page. See [Login Problems](/docs/troubleshooting/login-problems).
