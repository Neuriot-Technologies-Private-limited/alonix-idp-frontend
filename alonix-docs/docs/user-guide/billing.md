# Billing

## Overview

On **SaaS** deployments, **Billing** lets **Company Admins** view subscription plans, payment methods, invoices, and usage-related limits. Self-hosted customers manage licensing outside the application and may not see this section.

## Purpose

Keep subscription active, forecast costs, and resolve payment issues before they interrupt service.

## Prerequisites

- **Company Admin** role
- SaaS deployment (Billing menu not shown on all self-hosted installs)

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. Open Billing

1. Log in as Company Admin.
2. Click **Billing** in the sidebar.

[Screenshot – Billing page showing current plan, renewal date, and payment method]

### 2. Review current plan

Check:

- Plan name and tier
- Renewal or billing cycle date
- Seat or usage limits (users, storage, documents)
- Feature entitlements

### 3. Update payment method

1. Click **Payment method** or **Update card**.
2. Enter new card or billing details in the secure payment form.
3. Save. Future charges use the updated method.

### 4. Download invoices

1. Open **Invoices** or **Billing history**.
2. Select a billing period.
3. Download PDF for finance or accounts payable.

### 5. Change plan (if self-service)

1. Click **Change plan** or **Upgrade**.
2. Compare tiers and limits.
3. Confirm change—proration rules display before commit.

Contact Alonix sales or support for enterprise contract changes not available self-service.

## Expected Results

- Accurate subscription status and renewal dates.
- Successful payment processing without service interruption.
- Downloadable invoices for accounting records.

## Tips

:::note Self-hosted deployments
If you do not see Billing, your organization likely uses enterprise licensing—contact your account manager.
:::

- Set calendar reminders before renewal for budget approval.
- Monitor seat usage before inviting large user batches.

## Best Practices

- Use a corporate card or invoice billing per finance policy.
- Restrict Billing access to Company Admins only—already enforced by role.
- Archive invoices in your ERP system monthly.

## Common Mistakes

- Letting corporate card expire without updating payment method.
- Exceeding plan limits unexpectedly—review usage before bulk onboarding.
- Assuming Group Admins can fix billing—they cannot.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Billing menu missing | Not SaaS or not Company Admin. |
| Payment failed | Update card; check with bank for blocks. |
| Wrong invoice details | Update billing address in Billing settings; contact support for past invoices. |
| Plan limit exceeded | Upgrade plan or remove inactive users. |
