# Tutorial: Create Your First Workspace

## Overview

This hands-on tutorial walks **Group Admins** and **Company Admins** through creating a workspace, naming it clearly, and verifying it appears in the workspace switcher—ready for documents and team invites.

## Purpose

Complete a real workspace setup in under ten minutes as practice before production rollout.

## Prerequisites

- Company Admin or Group Admin account
- Logged in to Alonix
- A naming convention agreed with your team (optional but helpful)

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Plan the workspace

Decide:

- **Name** — e.g., `Marketing – Campaign Assets`
- **Purpose** — who will upload and who will search
- **Sensitivity** — whether this needs tighter membership

### Step 2: Create the workspace

1. Open **Groups** / **Workspaces** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Create workspace**.
3. Enter the name and short description.
4. Click **Create**.

[Screenshot – Completed create workspace form]

### Step 3: Select the new workspace

1. Open the **workspace switcher** in the top navigation.
2. Click your new workspace name.
3. Open **Dashboard**—counts should be zero or minimal.

### Step 4: Smoke test

1. Go to **Documents**.
2. Confirm empty vault (or welcome state).
3. Optionally upload one test file—see [Upload Document](/docs/tutorials/upload-document).

### Step 5: Invite a colleague (optional)

1. Open **Users** → **Invite user**.
2. Add a trusted colleague as Search User.
3. Ask them to confirm they see the workspace in their switcher.

## Expected Results

- Workspace visible in switcher for you (and invitee if Step 5 completed).
- Dashboard scoped to new workspace.
- Ready for document uploads and connector configuration.

## Tips

Create a `-sandbox` workspace for training before production data.

## Best Practices

Read [Workspace Governance](/docs/best-practices/workspace-governance) before creating many workspaces.

## Common Mistakes

Skipping workspace selection before uploading test files.

## Troubleshooting

Cannot create? Confirm admin role with Company Admin.
