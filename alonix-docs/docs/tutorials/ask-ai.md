# Tutorial: Ask AI a Question

## Overview

Practice asking Alonix AI Chat a question you already know the answer to—then verify the response against document citations.

## Purpose

Learn effective prompting and citation verification habits for daily use.

## Prerequisites

- At least one **Complete** document in workspace
- [Upload Document](/docs/tutorials/upload-document) completed or equivalent

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Pick a known fact

From your uploaded sample, choose a fact (date, amount, party name).

### Step 2: Open AI Chat

1. Confirm workspace in switcher.
2. Click **AI Chat**.

[Screenshot – AI Chat with sample question and response]

### Step 3: Ask precisely

Example: "What is the total amount on the Tutorial Sample Invoice?"

Avoid: "Tell me about the document."

### Step 4: Read answer and citations

1. Read the AI reply.
2. Click each citation.
3. Confirm text in source matches the answer.

### Step 5: Ask a follow-up

Example: "Who is the vendor on that invoice?"

Check citations again.

### Step 6: Try an unanswerable question

Ask about something not in your vault. Note how Alonix responds when no sources exist.

## Expected Results

Cited correct answers for in-scope questions; honest limits when data is missing.

## Tips

Specific questions outperform vague prompts.

## Best Practices

Always verify citations for decisions with business impact.

## Common Mistakes

Skipping citation clicks and trusting paraphrases blindly.
